🎓 RailKernel OS-to-Railway Architectural Mapping Guide

Quick reference guide for ITEC 55A project demonstration & defense

1. CPU Core ➔ Central Interlocking Junction

Only one train consist can actively cross the Central Interlocking diamond junction at a time, just like a single-core CPU executes one process at a time.

2. Process (PCB) ➔ Active Train Consist

Each train has a Process Control Block storing its PID, 5-state lifecycle status, Priority, Burst Time (ticks needed to clear the junction), and telemetry registers.

3. I/O Wait (Blocked) ➔ Station Passenger Boarding

When a train pulls into the Passenger Station siding to board commuters, it yields the main track (CPU) so ready trains behind it can be dispatched without stalling traffic.

4. Context Switch ➔ Track Switch Reassignment

When a train's Round Robin time quantum expires or it blocks for station boarding, the kernel saves its track sector register (`PC`) and switches signals to the next train.

5. Threads (TCB) ➔ Onboard Train Subsystems

Inside each train process, 3 lightweight threads share onboard power: Propulsion (active while Running), Automated Doors (active during Station I/O Wait), and Comms.

6. Deadlock ➔ Single-Track Circular Gridlock

Occurs when two opposing trains hold track segments and block each other nose-to-nose. Resolved via Kernel Preemption (backing one train into the station siding).
